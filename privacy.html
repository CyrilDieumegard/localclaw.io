LocalClaw account service

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This policy explains the data used by the LocalClaw website and the optional My Machines account workspace.

Account data

When you sign in with Google, LocalClaw receives the basic identity information needed to create your account: your name, email address, profile image and Google account identifier. Google does not provide your password to LocalClaw.

Hardware profiles

My Machines stores only the hardware details you choose to enter, such as a machine name, operating system, processor, GPU, RAM, VRAM, workload and optimization preference. These details are used to calculate and display compatible local AI models.

Sessions and security

Session tokens, IP addresses and browser user-agent information may be processed to keep accounts signed in and protect the service. Sessions expire after 30 days unless renewed through continued use. Signing out invalidates the active browser session.

Website analytics

Public LocalClaw pages use DataFast and Microsoft Clarity to understand page visits, referrals, devices and interactions. These services process analytics data under their own privacy terms. The account workspace is not used to collect prompts, local files, device serial numbers or operating-system credentials.

Infrastructure and service providers

  • Google provides account authentication.
  • Cloudflare Pages, Functions and D1 host the website, account API and account database.
  • DataFast and Microsoft Clarity provide analytics on public website pages.

Retention and deletion

Account and machine data remain stored while the account is active. To request access, correction or deletion of an account and its saved machines, email helplocalclaw@gmail.com from the address associated with the account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to helplocalclaw@gmail.com.
