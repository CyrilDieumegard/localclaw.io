AnimateDiff SD 1.5 local guide

Lightweight motion modules that animate Stable Diffusion 1.5 models on mainstream local GPUs.

Open installation sourceCompare video models

What it does

Text To VideoImage AnimationCamera Motion

The SD 1.5 motion-module path is substantially lighter than the documented 13 GB SDXL beta and is widely usable around 8 GB VRAM.

Hardware figures are practical entry floors, not performance guarantees. Resolution, duration, precision, offloading and runtime versions can materially change memory use.

Strengths

  • Works with many SD 1.5 checkpoints
  • Camera-motion LoRAs
  • Large ComfyUI ecosystem

Limits to know

  • Short clips
  • Visible flicker and lower temporal consistency than newer video transformers
