What it does

Text To Video

The official project reports 8.19 GB VRAM and recommends 480p for the most stable output from the 1.3B model.

Hardware figures are practical entry floors, not performance guarantees. Resolution, duration, precision, offloading and runtime versions can materially change memory use.

Strengths

  • Diffusers and ComfyUI support
  • Open weights and inference code

Limits to know

  • 480p is the recommended quality tier
  • Generation remains slow on entry-level cards
