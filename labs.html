How local inference works

You download a model after clicking Load model. Your browser stores its weights locally, then runs it using your GPU when available, or your CPU. The first download can take several minutes; loading it again uses the cache if your browser has kept it. Keep this tab open while the model is running.

Labs does not send your prompts or answers to an AI service and does not run session recordings. Conversations live only in this tab and disappear when you reload. The site and model host still receive normal network requests to deliver their files. See our privacy policy.

Both models use Q4_K_M quantization: Qwen3 0.6B, 397 MB and Qwen3 1.7B, 1.11 GB, under Apache 2.0. The local engine is wllama 3.6.1 (MIT). Downloads use fixed model revisions.

The game’s case notes and solution are authored; the interviews, remixes and chat replies are generated live. Tiny models can invent details, so use the case notes to solve the mystery. Measured timings describe this browser session, not desktop app performance.
