# Hardware guide hero images

The Mac Studio, Mac mini, and MacBook Air hero images were created for LocalClaw with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on August 18, 2026. Existing site product references were used to preserve the hardware category and pose, then placed in a consistent dark LocalClaw studio treatment.

- `mac-studio-dark.jpg`: generated dark-studio Mac Studio category image.
- `mac-mini-dark.jpg`: generated dark-studio Mac mini category image.
- `macbook-air-dark.jpg`: generated dark-studio MacBook Air category image with an abstract local AI interface.
- `macbook-pro-dark.jpg`: local derivative of the existing `images/computers/macbook-pro-m5.jpg` asset.

The generated images contain no added logos, readable product claims, people, or watermarks. They are illustrative category images and are not official Apple photography.
