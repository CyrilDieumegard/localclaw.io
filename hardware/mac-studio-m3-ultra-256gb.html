How this order works

The shared LocalClaw engine first rejects hosted-only, excluded and oversized records. It reserves system and 8k-context headroom, labels comfortable, good and tight fits, then ranks the remaining models by hardware fit, use case, catalogue capability ratings, runtime and freshness. Community stars are never included. This is practical guidance, not a standardized third-party benchmark.
