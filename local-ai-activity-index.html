                Methodology · Version 1.0 beta

                A useful signal, with a visible boundary.

                LocalClaw Atlas is being built in public. The beta starts with a transparent measure we actually have: anonymous, aggregated interest in LocalClaw by country, with worldwide administrative drill-downs and privacy-thresholded regional values where DataFast can publish them. Verified installations and active local model runs will only appear after a separate, opt-in telemetry system exists.

                What counts today?

                A country-level unique website visitor reported by DataFast during the 30-day window. Where DataFast supplies a country-filtered region breakdown, Atlas may also publish a subnational aggregate. Country, subnational, and city-cluster counts are separate provider breakdowns; city clusters are never used to derive region, county, prefecture, or LGA totals.

                What does not count?

                No installation, model launch, prompt, inference, machine identity, or claim about the total number of local AI users is included in this beta.

                How are locations ranked?

                Countries and published regional rows are ordered only from their own DataFast breakdown. No city coordinate, polygon area, population share, or parent remainder is used to estimate a region. DataFast currently has no county, prefecture, or local-government-area dimension, so those deeper boundaries are never colored or ranked. A beacon appears only for a separate published DataFast city cluster with at least five signals, at an approximate network-city centroid from GeoNames.

                How is privacy handled?

                The five-signal threshold is applied independently to country, subnational-region, and city-cluster rows before public JSON publication. Rows below five are omitted; only aggregate metadata is retained. Public outputs never contain individual people, devices, exact IP addresses, or exact visitor coordinates.

                Source and coverage: aggregated anonymous LocalClaw website traffic measured by DataFast, 31 Jul–29 Aug 2026, Europe/Zurich. Global country boundaries use Natural Earth 1:50m; worldwide internal subdivisions use Natural Earth 5.1.1 at 1:10m. Deeper U.S. county boundaries use the official U.S. Census Bureau 2025 Cartographic Boundary File at 1:5m. China prefecture-level divisions use the OCHA/HDX COD-AB 2020 dataset, licensed under CC BY 3.0 IGO; that source explicitly describes its China boundaries as unofficial, with unknown establishment date, and no endorsement is implied. Australian LGA boundaries are based on the official ABS ASGS Edition 3 Local Government Areas 2025, licensed under CC BY 4.0. ABS states that LGAs are statistical Mesh Block approximations of gazetted boundaries, not official legal boundaries. Natural Earth sometimes mixes administrative levels, so boundaries are cartographic references rather than a claim of identical constitutional status. Boundary display and naming are not a statement on sovereignty. Published beacon centroids use approximate network-city locations from GeoNames, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Source coverage is biased toward people and network routes that reach LocalClaw.

                Questions, answered plainly

                What the index can—and cannot—tell you.

                Which country leads the current snapshot?

                The United States leads this 30-day LocalClaw interest snapshot with 1,309 signals. That result reflects this dataset, not all local AI activity worldwide.

                What does “local AI” mean here?

                AI models designed to run on personal devices or privately controlled hardware instead of relying entirely on a third-party inference API.

                Which local AI models are people exploring in each country?

                Open the Models view to see the leading published local AI brand for each eligible country, then select its logo for model-level detail. The ranking counts privacy-thresholded visitors to LocalClaw model pages, not verified model use.

                Is this a local AI adoption ranking?

                Not yet. Adoption requires trustworthy installation or active-use signals and a defensible population denominator. The beta deliberately reports interest instead.

                What comes next?

                With explicit opt-in telemetry, Atlas can add verified installed and active views, then compare raw activity with a separate per-capita measure.

                Can I explore states, provinces, and federal subjects?

                Yes. Select any mapped country directly on the globe. In the United States, select a state again to open counties; in China, select a province-level region to open prefecture-level divisions; in Australia, select a state or territory to open its local government areas where the official source has real additional features. These deeper boundaries load on demand and remain neutral because DataFast does not publish county, prefecture, or LGA totals. Beacons remain separate city clusters, never verified residence or individual locations.
