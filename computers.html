Compare current Apple Silicon, AMD Ryzen AI Max, NVIDIA GeForce RTX, and GB10 computers. LocalClaw prioritizes usable AI memory, runtime support, and real form factor—not marketing TOPS.

Everyday local AI 24–32GB unified or 12–16GB VRAM Great for assistants, coding, RAG, image generation, and compact quantized models. Avoid buying an 8GB GPU for a new AI-focused machine.
Best long-term value 48–64GB unified or 24–32GB VRAM The practical sweet spot for serious local AI, larger contexts, fast CUDA workflows, and stronger quantized models.
Large-model tier 96–128GB unified and above For 70B–120B-class quantized models, demanding multimodal workflows, concurrent agents, and maximum local headroom.

Memory is compared honestly: Apple and Ryzen AI Max cards use unified memory, while GeForce cards use dedicated VRAM for the model-fit calculation. A PC may offload a larger model into system RAM, but it will usually run much slower.
