Is the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB Good for Local LLMs?

A practical RTX 5060 Ti 16GB local LLM guide: model tier, limitations, system RAM pairing, and when an RTX 5070 Ti or 24GB card makes more sense.

Quick answer

RTX 5060 Ti · 16GB VRAM

It is a practical new-card starting point when local LLMs are the priority. The important part is the 16GB version: it gives materially more model room than an 8GB card while keeping access to NVIDIA CUDA runtimes.

Good fit for

  • 7B and 9B-class quantized models
  • Some 14B-class models depending on quantization and context
  • CUDA-accelerated chat, coding and image workflows

Know the limits

  • 16GB still caps larger models and long contexts
  • Raw speed is below higher-tier cards
  • Confirm the listing is the 16GB version
